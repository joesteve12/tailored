How it plays

0.0s

Mark lands

Clay tile scales up from 70% and fades in over the dark ground. This frame is also your native launch screen, so there's no flash before Flutter starts.

0.7s

Name resolves

“dinkee” and the tagline rise in beneath the tile. This is the held brand moment — the screenshot you'd use in the store listing.

2.3s

Stitch runs

A needle draws a live terracotta stitch under the wordmark, looping for as long as the app needs to load — repositories, session, first data.

~5.3s

Handoff

Once ready, the whole stage lifts a touch and fades, revealing home_tab_screen. If loading finishes early, it just holds the mark and skips straight to the fade.
